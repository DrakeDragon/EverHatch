# Everhatch: Raise Your Legend

A gift game for your phone. You find one warm egg in the ruins of an old aerie, and you raise whatever hatches from it. It grows, learns, explores, makes friends and, if you care for it, becomes something legendary.

It is a single web page: no download, no account, no ads. It saves automatically on your device.

---


## Choose your egg

Your first screen asks which egg you want. Each kind is raised the same way but has its own body, egg, nest colors, six coat colors, stage names, special strength, tricks, story events and wild kin.

| Kind | Stages | Special strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Griffin** | Hatchling, Fledgling, Juvenile, Legend, Ascendant | Rarer treasure finds, extra provisions |
| **Hippogriff** | Eagle front, horse back, with its own stage names | Wild creatures befriend it faster, bond grows quicker |
| **Phoenix** | Cinder, Kindling, Blaze, Legend, Ascendant | More Starlight; **Rebirth**: once per sky ride it rises again with about half its energy |
| **Pegasus** | Foal, Yearling, Skyrunner, Legend, Ascendant | Better expeditions; tires about 15% slower in flight; rings are easier to catch |
| **Small wyvern** | Hatchling, Whelp, Drake, Legend, Ascendant | Takes about 20% less damage in flight; extra provisions |

You can switch kinds later without losing anything: Settings, then "Switch kind / new egg" shelves your current nest, and you can bring it back from Settings.

---

## What you do

**Care.** Feed, preen, rest, pet your creature (it has a favorite spot). Hunger, plumage or scales, rest and joy all matter. Its personality (bold, gentle, fierce or mischievous) grows from how you treat it.

**Grow.** Five stages: the last is **Ascendant**, a rare form above Legend with its own body and halo. Choose a path, build nine skills, and learn **23 tricks**, including special ones for each kind.

**Bond.** A ladder of bond levels unlocks petting, preening, riding and deeper story. Spend Starlight on **15 boons** that kindle permanent strengths.

**Explore.** Discover **20 regions**, from windswept ridges to the Cloud Sea and the Heart of the Sky. Each has landmarks, little stories and a unique relic. Send your creature on timed expeditions, or take a **sky ride**: hold to climb, release to dive, collect rings and gems, avoid storms and hawks, and race wild kin.

**Befriend the wild.** Meet **11 wild creatures**, each with its own temperament. Feed, play, spar or give gifts. Reach the top friendship tier and you can court one, which lays a new egg (the same kind as yours) that starts the next generation.

**Collect.** Seasonal feathers, relics and legendary items, each with a short description. Feathers are named for your kind: plumes for griffins, embers for phoenixes, mane hair for pegasus and hippogriffs, scales for wyverns.

**Build.** Upgrade the nest through several tiers, decorate it, and outfit your creature with gear.

**The world moves.** Real seasons change what you find. Festivals appear on special days. A daily moment and a visit streak reward coming back.

**Story.** A journal records what happens. Each kind has its own story events. There are **43 deeds** to earn.

**Share.** Make a picture card of your creature and send it to a friend.

## The Bestiary: real history and stats

The Story tab has two pages beyond the journal.

**Lore.** A full reference for all five creatures, with a stat tag on every line:
- Fact: documented history or measured science.
- Legend: what the old stories say.
- Game: how it works in Everhatch.

For each creature you get:
- A field guide of dozens of stats.
- A dated timeline.
- "Did you know" facts that unlock as your bond grows.
- Symbols, heraldry and places.
- Stories retold in short form.
- Names in other languages.
- Real-world kin with measurements, such as golden eagle, harpy eagle, peregrine falcon, lion, horse, grey heron, Komodo dragon, Quetzalcoatlus, flying dragon lizards and Rhamphorhynchus.
- Tappable links to sources.

A few things you will learn there:
- The griffin tales trace to a lost poem by Aristeas, passed down through Herodotus.
- The hippogriff has an inventor: Ludovico Ariosto, in *Orlando Furioso* (1516).
- The phoenix grew out of Egypt's Bennu, a heron-like bird.
- Pegasus is tied to the Hippocrene spring and to a constellation.
- A wyvern is, in heraldry, a two-legged dragon.

**Record.** 47 stats about your own creature: days together, growth, bond, nature scores, skills, tricks, expeditions, rides, races, regions explored, wild friends, treasures and nest.

## Honest notes

- The Bestiary facts come from web sources (Wikipedia and the scholarship and museum pages it points to, plus Theoi, the Met Museum, the Aberdeen Bestiary, Project Gutenberg and others), not from the ancient texts directly. Where sources disagree, the game says so. Check the linked sources if you want to cite anything.
- The griffin-fossil theory (Protoceratops) is shown as a contested idea, not a fact.
- The wyvern is drawn with four legs, so heraldically it is a small dragon.
- The perk numbers for each kind are design choices. The wyvern damage reduction and pegasus flight-fatigue effects were measured in tests; others are not tuned by long play.
- Only one shelved nest fits at a time.
- Progress lives in the browser on one device; use Save code to move it.

Made with care. Take your time with it. It will be there when you come back.
